DeepSeek-R1: innovation and geopolitics

Reasoning models, access to technology and international competition.

A major breakthrough has shaken the world of artificial intelligence with the launch of DeepSeek-R1, a large language model (LLM) developed by DeepSeek, a Chinese AI company founded in 2023 and supported by both state and private capital.

Origin and Strategic Goals

Origin:

DeepSeek-R1 emerged as part of China’s broader effort to reduce dependence on Western AI technologies. The model has strong ties with leading Chinese universities, such as Tsinghua University, and is financed by state-backed entities like the China Internet Investment Fund.

Strategic Objective:

The primary goal of DeepSeek-R1 is to develop AI models that combine technological sovereignty—minimizing the impact of sanctions—with global competitiveness, especially in emerging markets allied with China, such as Asia, Africa, and Latin America.

Key Features of DeepSeek-R1

One standout feature of DeepSeek-R1 is its open nature, allowing developers and companies to modify and customize the model to meet their specific needs. This approach is particularly relevant for:

  • Local applications, such as integration with Chinese dialects.
  • Industrial sectors, where tailored AI models are required.

Comparison with Other Models

Unlike fully proprietary models, DeepSeek-R1 promotes broader collaboration, showcasing China's adaptability in the ongoing battle for technological supremacy between the United States and China.
